## Replace these before launch

These are United States stock. YEPI is a South African programme, and the
people pictured have no connection to it. They are used purely as illustrative
imagery — no names, no quotes, no claim that they are participants. Swap them
for photographs of real South African founders, with consent, as soon as you
have them: drop the files in this folder and update `app/_content/people.ts`.
